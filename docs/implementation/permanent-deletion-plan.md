# Controlled contact deletion

Archive remains the ordinary removal action. The first permanent-deletion operation is deliberately limited to archived, unmerged contacts with no business references. It requires both current `contacts.read` and separately granted `contacts.delete_permanently` authority for that contact, an expected version, an explicit confirmation of the contact ID, and an idempotency key. It never deletes Auth identities, memberships, organizations, CRM records, activities, intake evidence or relationships. A generic conflict reports retained references without disclosing hidden records.

Delete the contact's owned identifier associations and unreferenced identifier registry rows atomically with the contact. Database foreign keys remain the final guard against concurrent or future module references; there is no cascading business deletion. Existing tags/relationships, including retained historical relationships, must be explicitly resolved or keep the contact archived. Merge sources, survivors with aliases and intake-linked contacts remain retained. This is contact-record deletion, not a claim of global personal-data erasure: independently retained business evidence and prior backups have their own lifecycle.

Retain a minimal deletion receipt containing stable ID, workspace, former owner membership, deleted version and timestamp. It supports current-scope checks on retries without retaining names, notes or identifiers. Invalidate and redact prior Contact operation replay payloads associated with that record, while retaining key tombstones so earlier requests cannot resurrect deleted data. Keep identifier-free audit attribution and the deletion event. No automatic purge is introduced.

Expose a browser confirmation and identical API/CLI/MCP operation. Test authorization separation, assigned scope, active/merged/referenced conflicts, concurrent edits/deletion/retries, rollback on late audit failure, orphan versus shared identifiers, stale replay rejection, retained unrelated records, and all four interfaces on fresh and upgraded installations.
