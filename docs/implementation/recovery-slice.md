# Recovery verification checkpoint

October 13, 2026. Format 5 implements manual encrypted capture, authenticated extraction, portable service images, stopped-service preservation, interrupted-operation recovery, frozen migration-source validation, exact role/grant restoration, explicit PGMQ data and sequence recovery, file integrity, session invalidation, unfinished-job quarantine and isolated loopback preview.

The final fixture archive contained 812,916,696 encrypted bytes. Capture took 49.942 seconds with 22.194 seconds of writer maintenance. Its isolated restore verified 103 tables and 18 files and quarantined nine unfinished jobs in 84.868 seconds. A second restore of the same archive after a partially failed update took 47.987 seconds. These are measured test datasets on the current machine, with other test activity, not capacity or availability guarantees.

Passed evidence:

- Real age 1.3.2 round trip; wrong key, truncation and tampering reject without published plaintext. Archive tests reject unsafe paths, links, duplicate entries, corruption and overwrite attempts.
- A late encryption failure preserves the previous archive, publishes no new output and restarts the exact original healthy services. A fault-injected status-storage failure cannot skip writer restart.
- SIGKILL during actual backup maintenance leaves a durable journal. `recover-services` restores exactly the original healthy writers without publishing a partial archive. Intentionally stopped Auth/web/worker containers remain stopped after ordinary capture.
- All eight distinct service images load into an initially empty, offline classic Docker engine with no access to the host image cache or registry. Format 5 fixes the verified containerd-index versus classic-config identity difference using unique archive tags and authenticated source/config digests; Compose uses the resolved immutable image IDs. The disposable engine and its anonymous data volume were removed after the test.
- Restored password login, stable workspace identity, old-token denial, CRM/Tasks/Files reads, file download digests and isolated browser access pass. Restored contact create/edit, replay, stale-version denial and audit pass. A real second identity accepts a limited invitation; hidden-contact access, write attempts and CRM access are denied. Atomic follow-up and queue insertion pass, including failed-transition rollback and replay without duplicate work. Workers and outbound mail stay disabled.
- Changed/missing applied migrations reject before backup publication. The checksum ledger records candidate source before execution. A controlled update commits A, fails B, corrects B and retries successfully; changing applied A remains rejected.
- A separate update-recovery case deliberately stops after A commits and B fails. Its exact pre-update archive restores the original schema/data to fresh resources. A remains applied and B absent in the preserved failed target; neither synthetic version exists in the recovered target. The restored business journey is repeated afterward.
- Exact database role reconciliation preserves grantor/admin/inherit/set flags and removes a revoked bootstrap membership. Built-in memberships omitted by `pg_dumpall` come from the explicit complete catalog snapshot. Queue content is verified byte for byte before quarantine; each file is checked against its length and SHA-256.
- Auth signing keys rotate; sessions, refresh tokens and pending one-time links are invalidated. Identity issuer mappings and account/password data remain intact. Invitation links tied to old signing material cannot be reused.

The primary installation's schema-23 pre-update archive contained 783,346,584 encrypted bytes, with 35.991 seconds of writer maintenance. Its isolated restore verified 96 tables in 62.611 seconds. The subsequent primary update to schema 28 preserved existing users, identity mappings, workspace and memberships exactly; Auth account IDs, password hashes and email confirmation also matched the recovered snapshot. The updated primary passed gateway/SSR, API/OpenAPI, CLI/MCP, readiness, private-route, role and PGMQ checks.

Reproduction tools: `recovery_archive_smoke.py`, `recovery_smoke.py`, `recovery_empty_engine_smoke.py`, `recovery_failure_smoke.py`, `recovery_interrupt_smoke.py`, `recovery_policy_smoke.py`, `migration_failure_probe.py` and `recovery_update_smoke.py`. Mutation probes require explicit disposable `*-identity-check` installations. Full application regression and independent standards/spec reviews passed; see [coverage](coverage.md) for remaining release gates.

Earlier experimental archive formats are deliberately unsupported. Raw diagnostic history is excluded; no automatic retention, schedule, off-machine copy or production promotion is claimed. A killed process may leave private staging files for operator inspection; the journal restores service availability, not automatic staging-file deletion.
